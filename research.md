# Static DR ads: health, longevity, online education (brief)
Sources: Meta Business Help "Creative best practices" / Meta Ad Library browsing patterns (MasterClass, Function Health, Noom, Hims, Oura, Coursera), Ogilvy/Caples/Schwartz DR canon, owner playbook rules/meta-ecom-playbook.md.
1. One idea, one focal point. Winning statics (MasterClass, Function Health) use a single face or single number plus one line. Thumb-stop happens in <1s; anything needing a second read loses.
2. Face + eye contact outperforms objects for instructor-led education (MasterClass instructor-portrait format has run for years in the Ad Library = proven).
3. Specific credential beats adjectives: "ranked #2" / "ahead of Bryan Johnson" > "expert". Specificity = credibility (Caples).
4. Offer clarity in the creative itself: price, date, format (live, Zoom, 60 min). Event ads that print date+price pre-qualify clicks, cutting cheap low-intent visits (our problem: visits with 0 carts).
5. Native, editorial look beats "ad" look in Feed (magazine covers, tickets, notes). Polished template banners (gold frames, stacked badges) read as ads and get scrolled.
6. Social proof as a small, real signal (stars + short quote), never the headline.
7. Legible at phone size: headline >= ~90px at 1080 width, max ~7 words; body text >= 30px.
8. 9:16: keep text out of top ~14% and bottom ~35% (Meta Reels/Stories UI); 4:5 is the Feed default.
9. Contrast and restraint: dark brand field + one accent color + real photo = premium; no more than 3 text sizes.
Applied: 3 concepts, each one idea; credential/number led; offer bar with course, $49/$79, Oct 27 7 PM ET and Nov 14 1 PM ET, Zoom, 60 min; Trustpilot line; single CTA in site button style.
